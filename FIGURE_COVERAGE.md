`surface/SURFACE_COMPARISONS.csv` retains the original column name `redistributed_fraction`. Figure S12C labels it **Normalized envelope-map difference (%)**. If occupancy maps are a and b, the displayed percentage is `100 * sum(abs(a-b)) / (sum(a)+sum(b))`. It compares spatial occupancy maps and is not simply the difference of their scalar volumes.

The clustering-sensitivity and ring-geometry tables (manuscript labels `tab:clustering` and `tab:ringgeometry`) are supported by `figure_revision/inputs/` and the independent recomputed CSVs. At fixed k=3, average linkage preserves all 78 pair relations at 10%, 20% and 30% thresholds; complete linkage preserves 53, 54 and 57 of 78 relations. Ring summaries are equal-trajectory means of the maximum single-residue motif occupancy per trajectory, including zero-motif trajectories; they are not the fraction of frames with any motif and are not interaction energies. No cluster count is interpreted as an equilibrium population.
